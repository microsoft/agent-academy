# Webinar Speaker Questions and Answers

Fictional training data for speaker preparation, not customer correspondence.

## Suggested audience questions

### What belongs in a useful handoff?

State the decision, the owner, the next action, and how the receiving team
will confirm acceptance. Include unresolved questions explicitly.

### What if an owner has not agreed to the work?

Label the assignment as proposed and obtain confirmation.
Do not treat silence as acceptance.

### Can we use a real customer example?

Not in this training scenario. Use a fictional or anonymized example only.

### Will there be a recording?

No recording decision is supplied. Record this as an open question rather
than promising a recording or follow-up link.

## Moderator guidance

Keep answers practical and avoid product pricing or sales claims.
The moderator has not been selected. Do not assign a real person.
