# Webinar Speaker Guidelines

Fictional training data. No speaker has been selected or confirmed.

## Speaker assignment

The Customer Success team must choose and confirm the speaker before the
February 4, 2027 readiness review. Refer to the role as "Speaker - to be confirmed"
until that decision is recorded.

## Required teaching points

- Explain the difference between a decision, an owner, and a next action.
- Walk through a handoff with one explicitly unresolved question.
- Show how the receiving team confirms acceptance.
- Finish with a practical checklist, not a product sales pitch.

## Preparation

- Prepare the chosen presentation format before the readiness review.
- Rehearse the full 45-minute running order, including questions.
- Supply readable examples and descriptive slide headings.
- Do not use real customer names, screenshots, or confidential project details.

## Delivery

Say dates and owners explicitly. Explain abbreviations on first use.
Read important example text aloud and pause before changing topics.
The moderator role is also unassigned; do not imply someone has accepted it.
