# Webinar Audience Research

Fictional training data for Zava's Better Project Handoffs webinar.
These are planning observations, not measured customer survey results.

## Intended audience

Project leads at existing customer organizations. They coordinate work across
teams and need a practical handoff method, not a product demonstration.

## Three recurring problems to address

- Decisions are recorded without a named owner.
- The receiving team cannot tell which next action is theirs.
- Acceptance criteria are buried in a long conversation.

## Learning outcomes

After the session, a participant should be able to:

- Record one decision, its owner, and its next action in a short handoff.
- State a clear acceptance check for the receiving team.
- Identify an unresolved question rather than treating an assumption as fact.
