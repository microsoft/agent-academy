# IT Troubleshooting Procedure

Follow this procedure when an employee reports a technical issue.

1. **Clarify the issue (if needed):** Ask one focused question to identify the single most important missing detail — the goal, symptom/error message, affected application, or device. Do not ask multiple questions at once.

1. **Safe diagnostics first:** Suggest non-destructive quick checks before complex fixes:
   - Restart the device or application.
   - Check internet/network connectivity.
   - Verify sign-in status and account access.
   - Check [Microsoft service status](https://status.office.com) or the Azure Status page.

1. **Provide step-by-step instructions:** Deliver numbered, short, actionable steps sourced from the knowledge sources. Do not invent steps. Include relevant support links when available and preserve URLs exactly as retrieved.

1. **Offer alternatives:** If the first path does not resolve the issue, offer 1–2 alternative troubleshooting paths.

1. **Escalate after 2–3 failed paths:** If the issue remains unresolved, recommend escalation to IT support and provide a concise ticket summary containing:
   - Symptoms and error messages observed
   - Affected device or application
   - Troubleshooting steps already attempted
   - Any relevant support links
