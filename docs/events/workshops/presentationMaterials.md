## Speaker Presentation Decks

Ready-to-deliver PowerPoint decks for facilitating Agent Academy workshops. Each deck is designed to be used as-is, just download, open, and present.

::: info These decks are for facilitators
These are structured to support live workshop delivery alongside the hands-on labs. Participants work through the curriculum on screen while you present. If you haven't already, read the [Facilitator Guide](./facilitatorGuide) before your first delivery.
:::

### Delivering Recruit NextGen

Use the NextGen Recruit deck with the [Recruit NextGen curriculum](/recruit-nextgen/), not the standard Recruit course. The deck includes a full-day agenda, concept slides, hands-on lab walkthroughs, and facilitator notes.

Have participants complete [Recruit NextGen setup](/recruit-nextgen/00-course-setup/) before the workshop. This path requires a Microsoft 365 Copilot license and Copilot Credits as well as access to Copilot Studio and SharePoint.

### Delivering Operative NextGen

Use the NextGen Operative deck with the [Operative NextGen curriculum](/operative-nextgen/), not the standard Operative course. The deck includes a full-day agenda, concept slides, hands-on lab walkthroughs, and facilitator notes.

Participants should have completed [Recruit NextGen](/recruit-nextgen/) and have a Dataverse environment, a Copilot Studio license or trial, permission to create solutions, agents, and workflows, and access to the Dataverse MCP server. Confirm Copilot Credits are available for building, testing, and evaluating the system. Review the [Operative NextGen prerequisites](/operative-nextgen/#prerequisites) and [Hiring Hub setup](/operative-nextgen/01-get-started/) before delivery. This is a preview course, so screens and steps may change.

::: tip Using the decks
These decks are designed to be presented alongside the hands-on lab content at [aka.ms/agent-academy](https://aka.ms/agent-academy). Participants follow the lab steps on their own machines while you walk through the slides. Use the agenda in your chosen deck for timing and the [Facilitator Guide](./facilitatorGuide) for delivery tips and troubleshooting.
:::
