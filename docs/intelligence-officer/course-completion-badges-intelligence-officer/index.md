# Intelligence Officer: Draft Badge Completion

<mission-meta />

## Mission Brief

You have connected Zava's customer recovery evidence to deterministic routing, reusable business skills, and responsible recommendations. Before declaring the field operation complete, review what your environment actually demonstrated.

**Badge claiming is not available.** This page is a draft evidence checklist, not a submission form or a promise of an issued badge.

## Review Your Evidence

1. Confirm **Zava Support Foundation** and **Zava Warranty & Goodwill Approval** exist with the expected publishers and components.
1. Confirm the custom Case table, four recovery tables, keys, relationships, and sample records match the course contracts.
1. Confirm the three persona roles match their matrices, and record that effective persona isolation was not established by administrator testing.
1. Confirm the routing plugin's local tests and scoped live checks passed, including partial updates and amount boundaries.
1. Confirm the summary and readiness skills preserve missing-data, Pending, chronology, and read-only safeguards.
1. Confirm the semantic-model view returns the two baseline products and the glossary tests use the actual sample records.
1. Confirm the follow-through planner and Advisor include the next-action handoff.
1. Confirm Cowork retrieved the intended skill and produced the correct REQ-3006 and REQ-3002 recommendations without changing or sending anything.
1. Record any unavailable preview exercise explicitly, including Skill Builder evaluation, multi-skill editing, version history, or Cowork access.

Keep screenshots and exported evidence privately within your organization's approved storage. Redact account names, tenant details, customer information, and authentication material before sharing.
